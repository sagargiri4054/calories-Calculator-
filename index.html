How to Use This Calorie Calculator?

Our advanced calorie calculator uses the highly accurate Mifflin-St Jeor equation to determine your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR). By factoring in your daily activity level, it calculates your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE). This tells you exactly how many calories you need to consume to maintain your current weight.

Understanding Your Results

  • Maintain Weight: The exact number of calories your body burns in a day. Consume this amount to stay at your current weight.
  • Lose Weight: A healthy caloric deficit (usually 500 calories less than maintenance) to help you lose about 0.5 kg per week.
  • Gain Weight: A caloric surplus to help you build muscle or gain weight safely.

Why is Calorie Tracking Important?

Whether your goal is fat loss, muscle gain, or simply living a healthier lifestyle, understanding your energy intake is the first step. Combining proper nutrition with a balanced exercise routine yields the best long-term results.
